# NeuArth Sustainable Consultants — GreenVolt Design System

## Overview
A human-crafted, editorial sustainability consulting interface inspired by `green-volt.framer.website`. Replaces dark sci-fi themes with warm, editorial architectural minimalism: a clean ivory canvas (`#F4F5F4`), deep forest green (`#1B4332`), crisp white cards, and full-screen clean-energy video.

## Key Sections & Layout (GreenVolt Architecture)
1. **Pill Floating Navbar**: Translucent white rounded bar with logo, page links, and high-contrast dark green pill CTA.
2. **Hero Section**:
   - Eyebrow pill: `// Sustainable Consulting & Building Simulations`
   - Expressive typography: `Build a Greener Tomorrow, Starting Today`
   - Clean description: `We leverage advanced building simulations to optimize environmental performance...`
   - Hero Video Container: High-impact curved card embedding `clean-energy.mov / .mp4` with subtle floating telemetry cards.
3. **Environmental Impact in Numbers (Stats Section)**:
   - High-impact editorial numbers: `100% Data-Driven`, `-40% EPI Peak Cut`, `NIT Gold Medalist`, `LEED • IGBC • GRIHA`.
4. **Comprehensive Solutions (Services Section)**:
   - 6 clean white cards with line icons, tags, and deliverable highlights.
5. **Recent Success Stories (Projects Section)**:
   - Notable projects (ASI Lothal, Barca Godrej Residential, Mahindra World City, Manipal University) in high-contrast editorial grid.
6. **Why Choose Us Section**:
   - Numbered step cards (`01`, `02`, `03`) explaining technical advantages.
7. **FAQ Accordion**:
   - Interactive expandable cards with + / - toggles.
8. **High-Impact Dark Green CTA Banner**:
   - Rich forest green (`#1B4332`) card with white button.
9. **Editorial Footer**:
   - Full business information, Hyderabad location, phone, email, hours, and copyright.
